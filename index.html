The People Behind The Work

Meet The Team

Decades of combined expertise, united by a passion for exceptional automotive craftsmanship.

Michele Liggieri

Classic & Performance Vehicle Specialist

Trained in Italy through a five-year electrical engineering apprenticeship with advanced studies in fuel injection and ignition systems, Michele brings over 30 years of hands-on expertise working with the world's most prestigious marques. His career spans full concours restorations, motorsport, and McLaren prototype development.

He has led complete restoration projects including an Aston Martin DB5 and iconic Ferraris — the F40, Daytona, Dino, and Testarossa — combining traditional craftsmanship with advanced diagnostic expertise.

Specialist Marques

Ferrari Lamborghini Aston Martin McLaren Maserati Rolls-Royce

Francisco Storani

Co-Founder & Classic Vehicle Technician

Combining a background in engineering and finance with a lifelong passion for classic automobiles, Francisco trained at the Heritage Skills Academy at Brooklands, specialising in classic vehicle mechanics, restoration, metal fabrication, and welding.

Alongside hands-on restoration work across Porsche, Alfa Romeo, Fiat, Jaguar, and Aston Martin, Francisco oversees the day-to-day management of the business — from customer relationships and project management through to marketing and operations.
